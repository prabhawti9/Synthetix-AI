## 🎨 For UI/UX & Product Designers
* **Design Standards:** Adhere to sleek, modern, highly intuitive, and accessible UI components with pixel-perfect execution.
* **Figma & Assets:** Ensure all design tokens, components, themes, and UI animations align with the official **Synthetix-AI Design System**.
* **User-Centric Motion:** Craft seamless micro-interactions and dynamic visual flow to deliver an elite user experience.

---

## 💻 For Coders & Software Engineers
* **Clean Code Paradigm:** Write self-documenting, modular, and strictly typed code.
* **Performance First:** Optimize bundle size, lower latency, and ensure maximum execution speed.
* **Component Architecture:** Build reusable, decoupled, and thoroughly tested UI and API components.

## 🧠 For AI Scientists & Machine Learning Engineers

## 🚀 The Contribution Process

### 1️⃣ Explore & Discuss
Understand the project roadmap and architecture. If you have a groundbreaking idea or feature proposal, share it with the core team first.

### 2️⃣ Pick a Module
Choose a component, UI screen, or feature module that matches your core expertise (Design, Code, or AI algorithms).

### 3️⃣ Build & Innovate
Craft your solution with the highest standards of quality, clean structure, and modern engineering practices.

### 4️⃣ Submit & Review
Submit your work to the repository. The core engineering leadership at **Synthetix-AI** and **Prabhavati Group** will review, test, and integrate your contribution into the main product.
